# Discovery review rubric

Use this rubric as an output quality check. Do not convert it into a numerical investment score or claim that passing it proves market demand.

| Dimension | Adequate output | If evidence is insufficient |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Names the startup, target decision, segment, and relevant period | State the unresolved scope and proceed provisionally where useful |
| Customer roles | Distinguishes user, payer, and relevant B2B buying roles | Identify the role that must be interviewed or verified |
| Problem | Describes a specific job, trigger, actual workflow, and consequence | Produce a problem hypothesis and discovery plan |
| Traceability | Links important findings to actual sources and locators | Label unsupported claims as assumptions or remove them |
| Sample | Counts distinct people and accounts and explains recruitment | State the available sample and limits without extrapolation |
| Contradictions | Preserves disconfirming evidence and alternative explanations | Ask what observation could falsify the current explanation |
| Quantities | Retains units, periods, denominators, and estimate status | Mark unknown values; do not fill them with invented benchmarks |
| Commitments | Separates interest, scheduled participation, signed terms, and payment | Report the actual commitment status |
| Recommendation | Explains why one segment or problem deserves the next test | Recommend a discriminating experiment instead of a confident selection |
| Next work | Includes actionable tasks and experiment interpretation rules | Specify the decision and evidence the next action should produce |
| Review state | Distinguishes generated output from human acceptance | Leave the result as a draft or recommendation awaiting review |

## Final check

1. Confirm that every purported interview, quotation, observation, and commitment exists in the supplied or retrieved evidence.
2. Confirm that founder assumptions and prior AI text were not promoted into customer evidence.
3. Confirm that a customer statement about cost or frequency remains attributed and qualified.
4. Confirm that missing notes, unavailable files, or unasked questions are not treated as negative findings.
5. Confirm that the recommendation states its strongest uncertainty and what would change it.
6. Confirm that the next actions fit the startup's capacity and natural usage or buying cycle.
7. Confirm that proposed outreach or shared-system changes were not reported as executed.

Choose a truthful output label: discovery plan, provisional customer hypothesis, evidence synthesis, or recommendation for founder/mentor review. Let the authorized human make and record any acceptance decision.
