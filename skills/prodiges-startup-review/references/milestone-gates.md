# Milestone review criteria

Adapt these prompts to the product, scope, and risk. They are not certification standards.

| Milestone | Inspect | Material gaps to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ready to test an offer | Defined customer/problem, actual source notes, relevant alternatives, offer hypothesis, eligible prospects | Customer and payer confused, no recent examples, inaccessible audience, unsupported difference |
| Ready for a paid pilot | Clear scope and price, demonstrated core workflow, understood limitations, payment/access path, data handling, support owner, measurable objective | Core result unavailable, unknown billing/access behavior, unclear terms, no support, data-access problem |
| Ready for public launch | Pilot outcomes, resolved critical failures, truthful assets, onboarding/billing evidence, cost/capacity limits, monitoring/support, pause/recovery path | Promise exceeds demonstrated behavior, critical journey failure, unsupported claims, no incident owner |
| Ready to increase acquisition | Channel fit, activation, repeat use at natural frequency, plausible economics, capacity | Scaling unresolved activation failure, incomparable cohorts, unknown heavy-usage costs, insufficient service capacity |

## Criterion assessment
Use demonstrated, partly_demonstrated, not_demonstrated, not_applicable, or contradictory_evidence. Give evidence IDs and a reason. Missing evidence is not proof of an implementation failure.

## Proposed decision
- proceed: evidence supports the bounded next step;
- proceed_with_conditions: name conditions, owners, timing, and supported scope;
- hold: identify a concrete blocker and resolution;
- insufficient_evidence: identify what is needed to judge.

Record a human decision separately from AI recommendations. Do not convert mentor acceptance of a document into business validation.

## Freshness and cohorts
A changed customer, offer, price, limit, architecture, or requirement may invalidate related work. Preserve history and identify only the affected reviews.
Assess each startup at its relevant stage. Avoid a single numerical leaderboard that rewards document volume or mixes different business models and missing data.
