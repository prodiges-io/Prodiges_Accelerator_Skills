LEGACY GEMINI GEMS OPTION
Prodiges Accelerator Skills v1.0.0 | 21 November 2026

Use this option only if your account still offers Gems instead of native Skills. For native Skills, use the ZIPs in gemini-zips/ and START_HERE.md.

1. Open Gemini and create a new Gem from the Gems page.
2. Use one of the four skill names as the Gem name.
3. Copy all text from the matching <skill-name>-instructions.txt into the Gem instructions.
4. Add the matching <skill-name>-guide.txt as a knowledge file.
5. Save, open a conversation with that Gem, and supply the startup brief and your task.
6. Repeat for any other skills you want to use.

The guide is a text adaptation for this interface, not a native installed Agent Skill. It does not add browsing, code execution, external integrations, or automatic handoffs. Give the next Gem the relevant prior report when continuing a workflow. Keep startup-specific evidence in that startup's conversation or approved workspace, not inside the shared reusable knowledge guide.

If the knowledge attachment is unavailable in your account, supply the matching guide in the conversation alongside the instructions; describe this as a conversation setup rather than an installed Gem.
